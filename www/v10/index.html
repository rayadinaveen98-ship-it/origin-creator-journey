ORIGIN ORIGINAL

The life behind the work.

ORIGIN invites you to experience a creator's life chronologically, beginning with the people and circumstances that shaped them. Our first featured journey follows Satyajit Ray from childhood into his filmmaking years.

V10 currently includes eight connected cinematic acts: The Boy, The Artist, The Dream, The First Film, Finding His Voice, The Wider World, The Final Years and The Legacy (1921–2026). Later periods are still available through the earlier V8 researched overviews, while we develop their cinematic chapters.
