# ORIGIN V10 — Act VII: The Final Years (1984–1992)

## Scope
Twelve connected, playable cinematic moments from Ghare Baire (1984) through Satyajit Ray's death on 23 April 1992. The story uses original symbolic HTML/CSS art, source-aware context panels, optional educational composition exercises and stable standalone browser-local progress. No hospital reenactment, private dialogue or actual film footage.

## Twelve historical moments
1. 1984 Ghare Baire and Tagore's Home and the World.
2. 1984 illness during Ghare Baire and Sandip Ray's completion following detailed instructions. Ray Society places heart complications in 1983; no unsourced diagnosis added.
3. 1985 Dadasaheb Phalke Award.
4. 1987 documentary Sukumar Ray, returning to the subject of his father.
5. 1987 Légion d'Honneur as dated by Ray Archive awards list; precise presentation scene deliberately not invented.
6. 1989 return to feature filmmaking following prolonged interruption.
7. 1989 Ganashatru adaptation of Ibsen.
8. 1990 Shakha Prashakha.
9. 1991 Agantuk, last completed feature.
10. March 1992 honorary Academy Award by video connection. Official Academy records index it as the **1991 (64th)** awards cycle but the ceremony happened in **1992**.
11. 1992 Bharat Ratna.
12. 23 April 1992 death in Calcutta.

## UX and safety

The contextual interpretation never masquerades as an actual image; no historic photograph rights assumed. Light and focus exercises are author-created, not Ray's cinematography.
