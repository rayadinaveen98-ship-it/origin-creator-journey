# ORIGIN V10 — Act IV: The First Film (1952–1955)

## Product goal
Deliver an emotionally coherent fourth cinematic act that follows Ray from attempting `Pather Panchali` to its first screenings. Preserve the existing welcome, homepage, three earlier acts, historical reader, Studio, and legacy URLs.

## Story scope — twelve scenes
1. 1952: assembling a largely inexperienced production crew (including cinematographer Subrata Mitra and art director Bansi Chandragupta).
2. 27 October 1952: first day shooting the kaash-grass train discovery scene.
3. Following Sunday: cattle have eaten the flowering kaash grass and delayed work.
4. 1952–53: casting a mixture of experienced and new actors and using Boral as a primary location.
5. 1953: borrowing, film footage as a proof of concept, and a difficult search for funding.
6. 1953: family's real involvement and Bijoya Ray's jewellery being pawned.
7. 1953–54: production manager Anil Choudhury's efforts and eventual West Bengal government financing.
8. 1954: filming resumes after a long hiatus, while state funds are delivered in instalments.
9. 1954–55: Monroe Wheeler / MoMA screenings proposal and John Huston's feedback on rough footage.
10. 1955: editing with Dulal Dutta and music by Ravi Shankar under intense deadline pressure.
11. May 1955: world premiere at MoMA in New York.
12. August 1955: release in Calcutta and the start of Ray's career as a director.
