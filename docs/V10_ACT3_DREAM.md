# ORIGIN V10.5 — Act III: The Dream (1943–1951)

## Release intent
A complete playable third cinematic act that continues from Act II while protecting V6, Story First, V7, V8, V9, V10 home and the original welcome animation. This is an incremental act, not the complete biography.

## Eleven connected scenes
1. April 1943 — junior visualiser, D. J. Keymer
2. 1943–46 — Signet Press and book jackets
3. 1944 — abridged Pather Panchali illustration commission and D. K. Gupta's film suggestion
4. 1947 — Calcutta Film Society (first screening: Battleship Potemkin)
5. 1948 — early film criticism and comparing imagined screenplays with finished films
6. 1948–49 — life with Bijoya, including documented 1948/1949 marriage dates
7. 1949 — encounter with Jean Renoir while he prepared The River
8. 1950 — work trip to London and extensive film viewing
9. 1950 — Bicycle Thieves confirms a practical idea for filmmaking
10. 1950 — development of Pather Panchali treatment during the return voyage
11. 1951 — a thematic transition from ambition into preproduction, not a claim of a precise event in that year
