# ORIGIN V10.4 — Act II: The Artist (1940–1943)

## Purpose
Expand immersive life-first storytelling beyond 1921–1939 Act I without redesigning the welcome animation or altering previous releases.

## Implemented
- Eight connected, full-viewport Act II moments: 1940 Santiniketan arrival, village sketching and observation, Nandalal Bose and Binode Behari Mukherjee, Indian and Asian art, travel to heritage sites, music/film theory, December 1942 departure, and April 1943 design employment.
- Act I completion leads directly to the new cinematic Act II at /v10/journey/act2/; Act II returns to Act I or moves into the existing V8 researched 1943 overview.
- Shared eight-act life map, with explicit CINEMATIC ACT vs RESEARCHED OVERVIEW states for unfinished later acts.
- Context drawer separating verified history, what the image truly represents, editorial interpretation and source links.
- One early-adult Wikimedia Commons Ray portrait (metadata approximately 1940–1943; uncertain date), correctly labeled as a period portrait, not a teacher photo or classroom scene.
- Two optional small-detail observation studies using ORIGINAL CSS illustrations.
- Separate local browser progress for Act II, with the V10 home resuming the newest Act rather than falling back to Act I.
- Cross-document visual transitions where supported, disabled when reduced-motion is requested.
