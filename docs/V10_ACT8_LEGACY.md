# ORIGIN V10 — Act VIII: The Legacy (1992–Present)

## Outcome
The final playable cinematic chapter returns ORIGIN to Calcutta, 1921, after ten source-grounded posthumous preservation and rediscovery moments. The complete narrative pilot has 87 scenes across eight cinematic acts. This is **not** the end of product development: archival rights, visual-documentary depth, real-device checks, independent history review and production polish are still open.

## Historical story
1. December 1992 — Academy-supported David Shepard inspection and preservation warning after Ray's death.
2. 1993 — London laboratory fire damages original Apu trilogy negatives, preserved for future repair.
3. September–December 1993 — first Ray Society meeting (7 September) and formal registration (14 December).
4. 2001 — Academy / Satyajit Ray Society restoration work on Seemabaddha.
5. Ongoing — archive of Ray's scripts, design, illustrations, letters and personal material.
6. 8 May 2015 — Apu Trilogy's 4K restorations return to cinemas, beginning at Film Forum.
7. 2021 — Ray's birth centenary and Academy Museum film retrospectives.
8. May 2025 — Aranyer Din Ratri's new restoration premieres at Cannes Classics. Film Heritage Foundation labels source film 1970; older Ray filmography can date production 1969.
9. October 2026 — SFFILM/SFMOMA announces Intimate Universes film series (4–21 Oct 2026). We deliberately do NOT claim particular screenings all happened or invent attendance.
10. 1921 → Now — editorial closing that returns to Act I's opening; not represented as historical footage or words said by Ray.

## Design and integrity
The new scene art uses original filmstrips, firelight, projected light, archival-paper geometry and a final circular motif that recalls Act I. On appropriate scenes, visitors can toggle a **simulated scratch/flicker cleanup on entirely fictional art**. The control is NOT a real restoration, before/after capture, scanned Ray frame, copyrighted footage, archive photograph, technical tutorial or substitute for preservation professionals.

Historical fact / creative interpretation / true image provenance are separately available in each scene context drawer. No invented private dialogue, voice impersonation, fake family picture or last words.
