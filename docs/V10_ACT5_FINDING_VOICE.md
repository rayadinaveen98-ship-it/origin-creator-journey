# ORIGIN V10 — Act V: Finding His Voice (1956–1969)

## Outcome
A fully navigable **thirteen-scene cinematic Act V**, linking directly from Act IV. Explores Ray's development after Pather Panchali — through intimate dramas, urban subjects, film music, colour, concentrated time and fantasy. This is NOT a licensed-film player nor a completion of the full 1921–1992 biography.

## Scene plan
1. Aparajito (1956), separation and growth.
2. Venice Golden Lion (1957), wider audience.
3. Parash Pathar (1958), fantasy satire and creative range.
4. Jalsaghar (1958), music, space and fading privilege.
5. Apur Sansar (1959), completing the Apu trilogy.
6. Devi (1960), belief and vulnerability.
7. Teen Kanya / Tagore documentary / composing / Sandesh (1961).
8. Kanchenjungha (1962), first colour film, first original screenplay and real-time structure.
9. Mahanagar (1963), working woman and Calcutta.
10. Charulata (1964), attention, emotional silence and concentrated time.
11. Nayak (1966), celebrity and inner doubt.
12. Goopy Gyne Bagha Byne (1968), fantasy, music and family storytelling traditions.
13. Aranyer Din Ratri (1969 in Ray Archive filmography), emerging contemporary themes.

We deliberately use the Ray Archive filmography convention for the Goopy (1968) and Aranyer Din Ratri (1969) listings even when other sources use theatrical release dates. No impossible event chronology, invented quotes or claims about Ray's private thoughts.

## Design and interaction
- Themed original abstract geometries on a cinematic film stage, different for distance, music, colour, city, fantasy and inward observation.
- On selected scenes, optional `Explore the craft` toggles framing distance, an original colour palette's saturation or *silent* visual rhythms. Each scene explicitly states these are NOT film stills, actual Ray lighting setups, exact colour grades, archival scores or copyrighted sounds.
- A Context drawer provides historical note, visual provenance, interpretation and links. No historical photo claims where no authentic photo is used.
- Dedicated scene progress key `origin_v10_act5_scene_v1`. Acts I–IV progress remains untouched. V10 homepage prefers the furthest reached act.
- Shared eight-act map now labels Acts I–V cinematic and Acts VI–VIII researched overviews; Act V completion hands off honestly to /life/#moment-12.
