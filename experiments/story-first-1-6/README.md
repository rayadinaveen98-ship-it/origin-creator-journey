# ORIGIN — Story-First Chapter 1.6

**Ray changes register: 1958–1959.** An additive eight-scene continuation from the Pather Panchali (1.4) and Aparajito (1.5) research prototypes.

## Start from source
```bash
python experiments/story-first-1-5/build_v15.py --base-file experiments/story-first-1-4/ORIGIN_Story_First_Chapter_1_4.html --output dist/ORIGIN_Story_First_Chapter_1_5.html
python experiments/story-first-1-6/build_v16.py --base-file dist/ORIGIN_Story_First_Chapter_1_5.html --output dist/ORIGIN_Story_First_Chapter_1_6.html
```
Open the resulting HTML. At the end of Pather Panchali, enter Aparajito; at its last act select **Enter part three**.

### Contents
- `chapter-16-section.html`: original cinematic SVG environments, markup, navigation, source drawers and notes
- `chapter-16.css`: responsive art direction and reduced-motion settings
- `chapter-16.js`: eight researched narrative acts and contextual experiments
- `build_v16.py`: deterministic additive builder that leaves 1.5 intact

### Eight scenes
1. The nonlinear 1956–59 filmography
2. Parash Pathar's fantasy satire and social status
3. Parash Pathar and Jalsaghar's production overlap and different genres
4. Jalsaghar's room as character; original lighting experiment
5. Sound and ritual; optional synthesized tones unrelated to the film's original music
6. Apur Sansar: Apu's adulthood, marriage, loss and fatherhood
7. Original framing and negative-space exercise
8. Recap and optional locally saved study note

Original vector environments are interpretive, **not historical photographs, film stills or Ray's storyboards**. Historical sources are linked at each scene. No film footage, Ravi Shankar/Vilayat Khan music, or voice impersonation is embedded.
